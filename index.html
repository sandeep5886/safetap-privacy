SafeSutra — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 October 2026

SafeSutra is a daily family check-in app. One tap each day tells your family you're safe. If you miss your check-in deadline, your family is alerted. This policy explains what information we collect, why, and what you can do about it.

App: SafeSutra (package com.safesutra.family)
Developer: Sandeep Srivastava, India
Contact: Vyaparhuman@gmail.com

1. Information we collect

Account information

  • Your phone number, shown to your family members so they can reach you. We don't use it for sign-in or to send you messages.
  • Your name, and optionally your city and relationship to family members shown on family rows.
  • If you sign in with Google or with an email code, we collect and store your email address to sign you in and to let you sign back into the same account.

Check-ins and family

  • The time and slot of each check-in you make, and your streak.
  • Which family group you belong to, and the members in it.

Medical information (only if you choose to provide it)

  • Blood group, allergies, conditions, and an emergency contact's name and phone number.
  • We collect this only after you give explicit consent in the app. It's used only to show family members or responders in an emergency, and it can be changed or removed at any time.

Location (only during an SOS)

  • Your location is captured only when you trigger an SOS and confirm it. Live sharing with your family stops automatically after 30 minutes.
  • We do not track your location continuously.
  • Where you were when the SOS started, and your last known location when it ended, are kept as part of that SOS's record for up to 90 days (the same period your check-in history is kept), so your family can look back at it. After that, this location data is permanently cleared.

Device and app data

  • A push notification token, so we can send you alerts.
  • Crash reports and error details, to help us fix bugs.
  • Usage events, such as tapping check-in or viewing the plans screen. These don't include medical details.

2. How we use information

  • To provide the check-in service, deadline reminders, and family alerts.
  • To send push notifications for missed check-ins and SOS alerts.
  • To diagnose crashes and improve the app.
  • To show your check-in history (up to 90 days) and monthly summaries.

We do not sell your personal information. We do not use it for advertising.

3. Who we share information with

We use these service providers to run SafeSutra:

  • Supabase – database, authentication, file storage, and our alert scheduler and SOS server.
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging (Google) – push notifications.
  • PostHog – product analytics (EU region).
  • Sentry – crash and error monitoring.

Family members see your name, your check-in status, and, during an SOS, your location and any medical information you chose to share.

4. How long we keep information

  • Check-in history is kept for up to 90 days, then removed.
  • Medical information and emergency contacts are kept until you remove them or delete your account.
  • SOS location data (where it started and where it ended) is kept for up to 90 days, then automatically cleared. The fact that an SOS happened, and who in your family acknowledged it and when, stays as part of your account's history.

5. Your choices

  • You can edit your name, city, and medical information in the app.
  • You can withdraw consent to medical information at any time.
  • You can turn off notifications in your phone settings.

6. Deleting your account

In the app: go to Profile → Delete account. Your account and personal data are deleted straight away.

Without the app: request deletion at https://ecjlwlqcqtsrmkwypkge.supabase.co/functions/v1/deletion-request. We send a code to the email address on your account, and delete the account only after you enter that code. You can also email Vyaparhuman@gmail.com. Requests made this way are completed within 30 days, except where we must keep data for legal reasons.

7. Children

SafeSutra is intended for adults. We don't knowingly collect information from children under 18.

8. Security

We protect your data with access controls, row-level security in our database, and encryption in transit. Medical information is protected by these controls and by the database's standard encryption at rest.

9. Changes to this policy

We'll update this page if our practices change, and update the "Last updated" date. Significant changes will be shown in the app.

10. Contact

For questions or requests, contact Vyaparhuman@gmail.com.
